Hi, I’m Sukrit — a statistics graduate curious about the patterns, questions, and stories hidden in data.

I have a master’s in Statistics from Hindu College, University of Delhi, and a background in statistical modeling, inference, and applied data analysis. My work has taken me from analyzing election manifestos and forecasting stock prices to investigating errors in image classification models.

I’m especially interested in natural language processing, time series, Bayesian statistics, deep learning, and making models more interpretable. Outside data, you’ll find me playing chess, swimming, cooking, or gaming.
